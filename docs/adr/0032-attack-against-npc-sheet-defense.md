# ADR 0032 — Offered attack resolves against the NPC sheet defense

**Status:** Accepted (rpg-42a.84, extended by rpg-42a.135). **Decision:** When a GM-offered attack names an NPC whose sheet holds defense and hit point traits for the pinned RulePack, the engine rolls the attack against that defense, rolls damage and commits a `DamageApplied` event in the same turn. With no usable sheet data the roll behaves as in ADR 0026: a check against the difficulty DC, nothing enforced.

**Context:** ADR 0026 rolled an offered attack as an ability check against a fixed DC and left defense and damage as follow-up work. NPC sheets (rpg-vbk) had no link to the roll path.

**Decision details:**
- The pack declares its combat data (`D20_LITE_COMBAT`: attack rule ids, defense trait id, hit point trait id, damage die). Core types carry no such field. A pack without it (dice-pool-lite, imported packs) rolls only.
- The sheet lives on the `npc` entity as `data.sheet`, either sparse or full (`damage.ts`, `NPC_SHEET_FIELD`). The engine parses it with `parseCharacterProfile`, requires its RulePack link to equal the campaign pack, and reads whole-number traits named by the pack. A missing, malformed or mismatched sheet means no combatant.
- The offer `target` is matched to one `npc` entity by id or by case-insensitive name. An unknown, non-NPC or ambiguous target means no combatant.
- Resolution reuses the pack's `attack` table: d20 plus attribute modifier, proficiency and flat modifiers against the defense, natural 20 doubles damage, natural 1 misses. Proficiency is listed as a flat modifier line so the roll card total adds up. The card target is the defense.
- `DamageApplied { targetId, amount, defeated }` follows `CheckResolved`, has `player` visibility so the GM packet carries it, and is skipped when the amount is zero. Current hit points are the sheet value minus the damage taken (`damageTakenOf`), folded over `DamageApplied` and `HealingApplied` events for that id in order: damage adds, healing subtracts, and the running total stays between zero and the maximum. Replay equals live state. Remaining hit points are never stored in a player-visible event.
- Only NPCs visible to the player (`public` or `player`) can be targets; a `gm` NPC rolls as a plain check. The roll card shows a public NPC's defense as the target, a deliberate tradeoff against `docs/04-character-sheets.md`.
- NPC attacks on the player (rpg-42a.135): a check proposal may carry `attacker`, an NPC id or name. For an attack rule the engine matches the attacker like a target (visible NPC only), reads its attributes from its sheet (a missing attribute counts as average), rolls its attack against the player character's defense and commits `DamageApplied` with the player character id as `targetId`. The player's defense and hit points are read from their character profile with the same `CombatRules` and the same `damageTakenOf`. When an attacker is named, the offer `target` is ignored. Conditions of the NPC, not the player, feed the modifiers. If the attacker or the player has no usable sheet, the roll is a plain check against the difficulty DC and nothing is enforced. A non-attack rule ignores `attacker`.
- Healing (rpg-42a.135): the GM proposes `heal { id, target, amount }`; `target` is a visible NPC or the player character (id or name). `healCombatant` (`healing.ts`) is the only writer: it commits `HealingApplied { targetId, amount }` with `player` visibility and refuses an amount that is not a whole number from 1 up to the target's missing hit points, so hit points never pass the maximum. A target without a usable sheet drops the proposal.
- A roll correction (ADR 0028 table fixes, rpg-42a.134) applies to an attack roll. The attack resolution stores `attack { targetId, damageBonus, damageRoll }`; `damageRoll` is the rolled damage die, or a spare die rolled after a miss so a miss can become a hit without a new random draw. The d20 pack corrector (`correctD20Roll`) re-resolves hit, miss, critical and fumble against the stored defense and returns the corrected `damage`. `applyOoc` commits `RollCorrected` and, in the same system turn, a `DamageApplied` whose signed `amount` is the corrected total minus the damage already applied for that roll (skipped when zero). The projection is unchanged: a sum, so replay equals live state, no damage counts twice, and undo hides the whole correction turn and restores the old damage. `defeated` is recomputed from the hit points before the attack, taken from the current sheet value plus the damage already applied, never from a stored value. A legacy attack resolution without `attack`, a dice-pool roll and a house-rule (`version`) roll are still refused.
- When the player rolls their own dice, only the attack die is reported (`roll-report.ts` keeps the first roll). Damage dice come from the engine rng.

**Consequences:**
- `introduceEntity` (`memory.ts`) writes `data.sheet` for an `npc` when the caller passes the campaign `CombatRules`. It refuses a sheet on another kind, without rules, malformed, for another RulePack, or without whole-number defense and hit point traits (`combatantOf` is the check). The GM entity proposal carries an optional `sheet`; `commitProposals` passes the campaign `CombatRules` (`combatRulesOf`, d20-lite only), and a refused sheet drops the whole proposal. The GM sheet is bounded and sparse-only (`motivations`, `background`, `traits`, `rulepack`; extra keys refuse the proposal); the engine sets `npcId`, `name` and `role` from the proposal. The GM packet carries one instruction line naming the sheet shape, the pack link and the defense and hit point trait ids from the `CombatRules`. A GM entity proposal for an npc that already exists sets or replaces its sheet through `setNpcSheet` (`memory.ts`, event `NpcSheetSet`, projected onto `data.sheet`, replay-safe) with the same checks as introduction; a refused sheet drops the proposal and keeps the old sheet. The opening-scene `commitProposals` call passes the campaign `CombatRules`, so a sheet is accepted there.
- `DamageApplied.amount` can be negative when a correction lowers damage. The player-visible resolution holds the damage die and damage bonus, not the remaining hit points.
- A damage die other than the pack default is not modelled. Healing has no pack rule: only the GM `heal` proposal restores hit points, and the NPC attacker's own sheet traits other than attributes (for example a weapon) do not change its attack.
- The outcome label on the roll card is the raw rule event (`attack.hit`), as for checks.
