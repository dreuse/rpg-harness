# ADR 0004 — Hybrid world agents + separate Narrative Director

**Status:** Accepted. **Decision:** Deterministic scheduler resolves routine simulation; optionally scheduled LLM actors make complex plans. Narrative Director controls revelation/pacing, not outcomes.

**Context:** A living world should evolve independently without overwhelming or punishing the player.

**Rationale:** Game-time scheduler, strict budgets, knowledge scoping, validation, idempotent commits and narrative attention limits. Independent axes for world autonomy and story intensity.

**Consequences:** No per-NPC always-on agent. No automatic offline clock advancement. No retcon to manufacture tension. Test both dynamic-world and relaxed-pacing combinations.
